01 · Urban Point

Visual reference sheet

Findings from urbanpoint.com, its hero phone render and three in-app screenshots (Explore, Food & Beverages list, Use Again, Savings). The App Store listing served no screenshots. Promotional framing (tilted mockups, oversized headlines, aqua backgrounds) is excluded.

Observed Estimated Proposed

Colour roles

Observed
  • White screens, near-black text, light grey tiles and chips (sampled ≈ #F5F5F5).
  • Red (sampled ≈ #FB1D26; site theme-colour #FF001F) only on: active tab, "View All", savings amounts, primary buttons, outline CTA.
  • Teal (sampled ≈ #139095) on utility icons: heart, gift, QR wallet, help, selected filter chip, month ticks.
  • Tints: mint #E3F6F4 behind teal icons; pink tint behind red "April" chip.

Typeface & hierarchy

Estimated

Rounded geometric sans, double-storey a, round dots, open g. Matches Gordita / Brandon-type faces. Closest Google font: Plus Jakarta Sans (substitution documented in Foundations).

  • Screen title ≈ 24–26 px bold (back-arrow row).
  • Greeting ≈ 24 px semibold; section header ≈ 18 px bold.
  • Row title 16–17 px bold; body 14 px medium; meta 13 px grey.
  • Tab label ≈ 12 px semibold; tight letter-spacing, no caps except "SAVE".

Spacing & alignment

Estimated
  • Screen gutter ≈ 20 px (390-wide frame).
  • Grid gap between tiles ≈ 12 px; section gap ≈ 24 px.
  • List row padding ≈ 16 px vertical; logo-to-text gap ≈ 16 px.
  • Left-aligned titles; back button and title sit on one 48 px row.
  • Dense: four list rows visible above the fold.

Corner radii & borders

Estimated
  • Category tiles ≈ 16 px; hero banner ≈ 24 px; offer cards ≈ 20 px.
  • Chips, buttons and floating controls fully round.
  • Borders 1 px light grey (≈ #E6E6E6) on cards; hairline separators on rows.
  • Outline CTA: 1.5 px red stroke, 12–14 px radius.

Shadows & elevation

Estimated
  • Almost flat. Cards rely on fill or 1 px border; a very soft shadow (≈ 0 2px 10px, 6% black) on offer cards.
  • Bottom navigation: white, hairline top border, no shadow.
  • Floating circular controls: light grey fill, no shadow over white; white fill over photography.

Icons

Observed
  • Outlined, rounded-joint icons, ≈ 1.8 px stroke at 24 px.
  • Teal for utility (heart, gift, QR), near-black for navigation, red for the active tab.
  • Small filled glyphs (pin, star) in meta rows at ≈ 14 px.

Image crops & masks

Observed
  • Cuisine categories: circular photo crops (≈ 76 px) with a 2-line label beneath.
  • Merchant logos: circles (≈ 80 px list, ≈ 72 px cards), white or brand fill.
  • Category tiles: isolated cut-out objects anchored bottom-right, text top-left.
  • Hero banner: full-bleed photo, dark overlay, centred white copy.

Navigation & card proportions

Estimated
  • Bottom bar ≈ 84 px incl. home indicator; 5 items, icon 24 px over 12 px label.
  • Category tiles: 2-up ≈ 165×115 px, 3-up ≈ 105×115 px.
  • Offer card ≈ 350×130 px with ticket notches on the right edge between heart and gift.
  • Hero banner ≈ 350×200 px.
  • Restaurant row ≈ 110 px tall; 80 px logo, 3 text lines + red offers link.

Accessibility departures

Proposed

Observed red (#FB1D26) and teal (#139095) reach only ≈ 3.9 : 1 on white. For text and icon-only controls the system uses #E3111B (4.8 : 1) and #0E7F84 (4.8 : 1); the observed values stay for large display use and brand marks. Secondary grey is set at #666666 (5.7 : 1) instead of the paler observed grey. Disabled labels keep grey/400 (#9A9A9A, 2.6 : 1) as an intentional exemption; hero copy sits on a 55% scrim (observed ≈ 40%) so white text clears 4.5 : 1 over mid-tone photography.

Not reproduced

Proposed

Savings streak, QR wallet, gift sharing, monthly reset and subscription messaging are Urban Point features outside LastTable's scope. Their styling (teal tick circles, pink status chip, outline CTA) is kept as component treatments where LastTable has an equivalent.
