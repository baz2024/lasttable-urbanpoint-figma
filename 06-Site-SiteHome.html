Questions diners ask

Is LastTable free for diners? ＋

Yes. You pay the restaurant directly for your order. You can browse without an account; you verify a mobile number only when you book.

What are Flash Tables? ＋

Time-limited offers a restaurant publishes — for example 20% off food or a free dessert — on a limited number of tables. The offer is applied to your bill at the restaurant.

What is Cancellation Radar? ＋

Ask Radar to watch a full restaurant for your party size and time window. When a table opens, it is held for you for five minutes and you get an alert in the app.

What does the restaurant see about me? ＋

Your name, phone number, booking details and any requests, plus whether you are new to that restaurant. Never your other bookings or preferences.

How do I join the Dublin pilot? ＋

Request an invite. Invites go out in small batches through TestFlight on iPhone and Google Play testing on Android.
