LastTable Business · design system
Same family as the diner app.
Built for the pass, not the sofa.
The diner app owns burgundy. Business keeps ink for its frame and uses teal for every action — so burgundy can mean exactly one thing on a staff screen: a Flash Table, what the diner sees. Shared with the diner app: Plus Jakarta Sans, the clock-on-table mark, radius 8/12/16/20, the card and float shadows, pill chips, outlined 1.8-stroke icons, and the grey/100 canvas.
