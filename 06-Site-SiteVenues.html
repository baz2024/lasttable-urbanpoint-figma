How it works

1Tell us what's free

Tap the times and tables you have tonight. Thirty seconds on a phone — no POS integration needed to start.

2Publish a Flash Table (optional)

10–25% off food or a free dessert, on exactly the tables you choose. Diners see the terms; you set the limit.

3Diners book, you confirm arrival

Bookings arrive with name, number, party size and whether they're new to you. Mark them seated at the door.

4Pay only for new diners who turn up

Credits are deducted per seated new diner. No-shows and regulars cost nothing.

What venues ask

Do I need to integrate my POS or booking system? ＋

No. You can start by tapping free times on a phone. Integrations with common booking systems are on the pilot roadmap.

Can I limit how many tables get the offer? ＋

Yes — the offer, the times and the number of tables are all yours to set, and you can switch a Flash Table off the moment you're full.

How do you know a diner is new to us? ＋

By verified mobile number. If that number has been seated at your venue before through LastTable, the booking is free.

What about no-shows? ＋

You confirm arrival at the door. Only seated new diners use credits; no-shows cost nothing and repeat no-show numbers are restricted.
